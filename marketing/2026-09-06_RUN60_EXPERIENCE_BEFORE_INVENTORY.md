# CityVerse Run 60 — Experience Before Inventory

## Customer Truth
CityVerse has a compelling multi-POV experience thesis, but a prototype becomes less credible when illustrative events, brands, prices, ratings, partnerships, or payment methods are presented like verified live inventory.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** Sell the feeling of being there — not inventory you have not verified.

**Body:** CityVerse is a prototype for multi-POV events, contextual commerce, AI guidance, CityTwin districts, creator workflows, and memory replay. The strongest story is the interaction model itself: choose a viewpoint, explore a moment, discover context, and understand how commerce could hand off when rights and integrations are real. Event rights, live feeds, merchant inventory, brand relationships, pricing, checkout, and fulfillment remain verification gates.

**CTA:** Explore the CityVerse experience model and tell us which event category or POV would be worth validating first.
