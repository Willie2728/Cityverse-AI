## Creative Strategy
### CV-TXT-009
**Hook:** Don't pitch “immersive engagement.” Scope one event moment, one dependency, and one receipt.

**Audience:** creators, brands, venues, event partners, and investors evaluating a CityVerse concept.

**CTA:** Define one first event test before sharing contact.

**Copy:**
A virtual event idea can sound impressive while still hiding the one thing that makes or breaks the test: what has to be true for it to run. Pick one event moment. Name the audience interaction you want to test. Then name the external dependency — rights, venue access, a live feed, commerce rails, or a partner integration — and the receipt that would make the result inspectable. CityVerse should earn the second step with one bounded test, not a broad promise about an entire virtual world.

Claims boundary: this is planning copy. It does not establish event rights, inventory, feed access, partner acceptance, transactions, or a production deployment.

## CV-DOC-005 — One Event Moment Pilot Card
1. Event moment — what single fan, creator, brand, or commerce moment is being tested?
2. Audience — who is the first intended participant?
3. Dependency — what rights, feed, venue, payment, or integration condition must exist?
4. Success signal — what observable behavior would make the test worth a second step?
5. Evidence receipt — what log, interaction receipt, transaction receipt, or rights/access confirmation would make the result inspectable?
6. Decision window — when will the buyer or partner decide whether to continue?
7. Human owner — who is accountable for the decision and dependency verification?

Decision rule: no second-step claim until the predeclared dependency is resolved and the required receipt exists.
