# RUN159 — CityVerse Event Extension Pilot

## Customer Truth
Current live-experience projects are emphasizing mixed-reality participation, multiple viewpoints, interactive fan experiences, and brand activations around bounded event moments. IBC2026's Immersive Festival Live accelerator is prototyping multi-perspective video, spatial audio, and two-way audience participation; iHeartMedia's 2026 House of Music is built around discrete interactive rooms and brand activations surrounding a live festival. The growth implication is to sell one testable event extension before the whole virtual-world vision. These are category examples, not CityVerse performance evidence.
