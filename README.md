# CITYVERSE AI

**Presented by Wilkerson Collective Labs**

CITYVERSE is Wilkerson Collective Labs' immersive live-presence city platform: a virtual-city environment designed to bring together live events, entertainment, commerce, digital real estate, AI-powered experiences, creator tools, education, gaming/adventure, collaboration, advertising, partnerships, and connected city experiences.

## Core Experience

CITYVERSE is designed around the participant's point of view: enter virtual cities, attend and experience events, shop in context, connect and collaborate, build and operate digital businesses, create content, interact with AI companions and services, participate in gaming/adventure experiences, acquire or use digital real estate, and engage with brands and partners.

## Platform Pillars

- Virtual Cities & CityTwin experiences
- Live Events: sports, concerts, racing, Broadway, fashion and entertainment
- Presence-based commerce and Shop The Moment experiences
- AI concierge, companions and intelligent services
- Digital real estate and business-building experiences
- Creator tools and creator economy
- Gaming, adventure and immersive experiences
- Education and collaboration
- Advertising, sponsorship and partner activations
- Membership / PresencePass experiences
